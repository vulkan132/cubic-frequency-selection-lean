# Proof drafts outside the audited theorem inventory

The four Work*.lean files are development copies whose final proofs were
split into the audited F19 GMZP0 modules. They are retained as drafting history.

The elementary F20 pair-geometry results were moved into the audited project
as `GMZP0/PairGeometry.lean` and `GMZP0/PairScale.lean`. The geometry draft is
retained as its development record. The former `F20Representations.lean`
was completed and promoted into `GMZP0/PairRepresentations.lean` at F21.
The exceptional-pair development draft was likewise promoted into
`GMZP0/ExceptionalPairs.lean`. Their checked results are in the F21 inventory.

The audited F20 preliminaries prove bounded exceptional-event deletion, the
actual pair-shift map's seventh-norm <=1, a<=256 from the actual local-four
bound, short shrunk radius, floor lower bounds, integer pair-shift range, no
extra modular collisions, and a scale threshold uniform before N. They do
not prove family concatenation or the representation/exceptional-pair counts.
F21 adds both counts and the conditional passage to positive real seven-cubes;
the explicit external concatenation input remains unproved.

F22 development drafts were completed and promoted to GlobalCubeCollision,
GlobalCubeThreshold and OriginalFiberRetention. Their final versions and the
uniform preparation wrapper are part of the F22 audited inventory.

F23 drafts were completed and promoted to FiniteSchur, BlockSchur,
FewLargeBlocks and OriginalBlockSchur. The 39 new declarations are all in
the F23 audited inventory. The promoted drafts were removed to avoid
maintaining two versions of the same proof.

F24 drafts were promoted to OperatorStability, AssignedOperator,
FiniteEnergyTriangle, MajorArcStability and FreezingStep. Their 31 new
declarations are in the F24 audited inventory; the duplicate drafts were removed.
